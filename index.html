Google email and calendar

Send from your Gmail. Keep your calendar in sync.

Connect your Google account and HoneyTabs sends the emails you write from your own address, so replies come straight back to you. Your event dates and invites go on your Google Calendar, and you can see who said yes.

  • Your address, your voiceSponsors and vendors see your name, not a no-reply address.
  • Invites with RSVPsSend event invites and see yes, no and maybe right in the app.

FAQ

Good questions

Don't see yours? Email hello@honeytabs.com and we'll get back to you.

When can I get HoneyTabs?

We're finishing testing now. HoneyTabs is coming soon to the App Store and Google Play. Email hello@honeytabs.com and we'll let you know the day it's ready, or get you in early.

Does it work on iPhone and Android?

Yes, both. Your team can use whatever phone they already have, and everyone sees the same plan. A version that runs in your web browser is in the works too.

Does HoneyTabs read my email?

No. When you connect Google, HoneyTabs can only send email you write and keep its own events on your calendar up to date. It can't open your inbox. Read how we handle Google data.

Can I choose what my team sees?

Yes. Team permissions let you decide who sees costs, files, contacts and more. A volunteer can see their tasks without seeing the budget.

Can it match our colors?

Yes. Pick your organization's brand color and the app uses it for your team.

Can I print from the app?

Yes. Print photos, PDFs and the production sheet to any printer your phone can reach.

Is my data sold or used for ads?

Never. There are no ads and no tracking in HoneyTabs. Your event data belongs to your team. See our Privacy Policy.
