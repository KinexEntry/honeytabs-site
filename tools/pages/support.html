How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to More → Delete account. It's permanent. You can also email {{email}} from the address on your account and we'll take care of it. Our Privacy Policy explains what happens to shared team data.

How do I disconnect Gmail and Google Calendar?

Open Settings in the app and disconnect your Google account. You can also remove access from your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/connections. HoneyTabs never reads your inbox. Here's exactly what it can do.

How do I add or remove teammates?

Owners and admins can invite people by email, set what each person can see and remove anyone from the team at any time.

Can I print from the app?

Yes. Open a photo, PDF or the production sheet and tap Print. It works with any printer your phone can reach.
